WHO WE ARE

Faith, Knowledge, and Service

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is an educational institution dedicated to providing quality Catholic education and meaningful learning experiences inspired by Christian faith and values.

Since its establishment in 1927, KCC has been part of the educational community of Kabankalan City. The institution values the development of students not only through academic learning but also through faith formation, discipline, leadership, and service to others.

  • Providing quality education guided by Catholic teachings.
  • Promoting Christian values, discipline, and moral responsibility.
  • Developing knowledgeable, responsible, and service-oriented individuals.

OUR HISTORY

A Legacy of Faith and Education Since 1927

Established in 1927, Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. has been part of the educational history of Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental. Its foundation reflects a commitment to Catholic education and the importance of developing individuals through knowledge and faith.

Throughout its years of service, KCC has continued to uphold the values of education, Christian formation, and community involvement. Its legacy represents the dedication of the school community to preparing learners for responsible and meaningful contributions to society.

WHAT WE VALUE

Our Educational Pillars

The principles that shape the educational mission and values of the KCC community.

Academic Excellence

KCC encourages students to pursue academic excellence by developing their knowledge, critical thinking, creativity, and skills through meaningful learning experiences.

Faith Formation

Guided by Catholic teachings, KCC promotes faith, spiritual growth, compassion, and moral values that help students become individuals of good character.

Community Service

KCC values community involvement by encouraging students to practice kindness, cooperation, volunteerism, and responsibility toward others.

Holistic Development

KCC supports the overall development of learners by nurturing their intellectual, spiritual, emotional, social, and personal abilities.

OUR IDENTITY, OUR MISSION

Teach Ye All Nations

"Catholic Education: Our Identity, Our Mission"

Inspired by the Catholic faith and the teachings of Jesus Christ, Kabankalan Catholic College is committed to forming learners who value knowledge, faith, integrity, and service. Through education, KCC aims to encourage every student to become a responsible member of the Church and society.
