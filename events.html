KCC Campus Life & Events

Discover the memorable events and activities of Kabankalan Catholic College. Experience a vibrant school community where students develop their talents, strengthen friendships, demonstrate leadership, and celebrate achievements together.

KCC Foundation Day Celebration

Celebrating the legacy and achievements of KCC

The KCC Foundation Day Celebration honors the institution's history, traditions, and accomplishments. It brings together students, faculty, staff, and alumni through various activities, performances, and meaningful celebrations that promote unity, gratitude, and school pride.

KCC Intramurals and Sports Festival

Promoting teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship

The KCC Intramurals and Sports Festival provides students with opportunities to showcase their athletic abilities and teamwork through different sporting competitions. The activity promotes sportsmanship, discipline, friendship, and unity among the KCC community.

Building Memories, Strengthening Community

At Kabankalan Catholic College, education goes beyond classroom learning. Every school activity provides opportunities for students to discover their talents, develop their leadership skills, build friendships, and practice the values of faith and service.
